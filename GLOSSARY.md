# Piedras y arena

A travel blog of bike trips, told stage by stage and placed on a map.

## Language

**Trip**:
One journey by bike, told as a sequence of stages, with its own colour.
_Avoid_: Route, tour, journey

**Stage**:
One post of a trip, usually one day's ride; it can also be a rest day or several days told together.
_Avoid_: Day, leg, post, entry

**Stage place**:
The named place where a stage starts; the stage's pin stands there. Its track can begin a few km away, after a transfer.
_Avoid_: Stage location, destination, end point

**Track**:
The road actually ridden on a stage, from its GPS recording; drawn on the map as a solid line.
_Avoid_: Route, GPX, path

**Trip line**:
The dashed line from a stage's place to the next stage's, drawn where the stage has no track; it shows the direction of travel, not the road ridden.
_Avoid_: Route, track, path
