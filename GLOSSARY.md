# Piedras y arena

A travel blog of bike trips, told stage by stage and placed on a map.

## Language

**Trip**:
One journey by bike, told as a sequence of stages, with its own colour.
_Avoid_: Route, tour, journey

**Stage**:
One post of a trip, usually one day's ride; it can also be a rest day or several days told together.
_Avoid_: Day, leg, post, entry

**Stage place**:
Where a stage starts; the stage's pin stands there.
_Avoid_: Stage location, destination, end point

**Trip line**:
The dashed line that joins a trip's stage places in order and shows the direction of travel; it is not the road ridden.
_Avoid_: Route, track, path
