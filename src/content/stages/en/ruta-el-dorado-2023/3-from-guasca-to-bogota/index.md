All that was left was the last day to get back to Bogotá. With coffee, fruit and biscuits... what could go wrong?

![](./IMG_20231106_071044.jpg)

I set off towards Guasca again to pick up, shortly after, the so-called Ruta Chingaza.

![](./IMG_20231106_081109.jpg)

This route crosses Chingaza National Natural Park, which lies close to Bogotá. The park has magnificent vegetation and gorgeous views.

![](./IMG_20231106_090420.jpg)

Or so I believe, since the rain that keeps the vegetation in bloom didn’t leave me for the whole park.

![](./IMG_20231106_093008.jpg)

After putting up with the drizzle for a good few hours, I reached La Calera, right at the spot where I’d stopped for coffee on the first stage.

![](./IMG_20231106_094915.jpg)

And if I’d liked the place so much, why not go back? But this time I was hungry: it had been quite a few kilometres and I fancied something more filling.

![](./IMG_20231106_104831.jpg)

A superb hot <em>tamal</em> to fill my belly and chase away the day’s cold.

![](./IMG_20231106_105226.jpg)

Although, to be fair, there was no need to chase away the cold, because as soon as I left La Calera I started climbing, climbing and climbing. It’s exactly what I did on the first day, but the other way round, and I didn’t remember having gone down so much. Descents and climbs just feel different.

After all that climbing, I reached the Alto de Patios again, and this time I barely stopped, because rain was looming over Bogotá.

![](./IMG_20231106_130002.jpg)

I thought I’d get away with it. Well, no, I didn’t. The rain caught me hard on the way down. I don’t know if I was getting wetter from the rain falling from the sky or from the spray off my wheels, but the truth is I got soaked.

The ride to the hotel was quite something. But still, it wasn’t very cold, so it wasn’t all that bad. Mind you, I had to take a photo in the hotel lift to capture the moment.

![](./IMG_20231106_133553.jpg)

What a shame, the route is over. But hey, next week I have another one, and an even longer one. I can’t wait to keep pedalling around Colombia!

![](./IMG_20231106_153051.jpg)
