I don’t want to go on too long in this post. I had never been on television, nor been interviewed by a magazine. But a call from my great friend Ruth Moll landed me, from one day to the next, on the shoot of a TV programme with <a title="Biography of Chus Castellanos" href="https://planetmtb.es/conocenos/" target="_blank">Chus Castellanos</a>, who, alongside his <a title="PlanetMTB website" href="http://www.planetmtb.es/" target="_blank">PlanetMTB</a> project, had taken on another one called Planeta Bike. Those were two ~~cold~~ great days of filming along the Camino de Santiago, the Way of St James, with Chus and his crew, and this was the result.
